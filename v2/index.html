HOW WE HELP

Buying, selling
or moving on?

Buyer advocacy

We locate, investigate and negotiate your property within your buying capacity, whether it's a family home, an investment or a development site. That includes off-market opportunities and full due diligence before you commit.

Vendor advocacy

We don't sell property. We represent you: selecting the most suitable agent for your home, then overseeing the campaign and the negotiation through to the result. Waterman developed Seller Advocacy in 2009, after years of vendors asking for it.

Downsizing

Working out what you need next, clearing and decluttering, then selling well and buying right. One team manages the whole move so it stays calm and well planned.

Property clearances

Living and deceased estates cleared across Adelaide, for executors, downsizers and families moving into aged care. Donations and recycling first, then removal, repairs and restoration ready for sale. Site inspection and quote are free.

For home buyers, investors, sellers, executors and downsizers.
